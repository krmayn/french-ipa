# French IPA Phonetic font

`french-ipa-phonetic.woff2` is a subset of **Charis SIL 6.101** (Regular) by SIL International, from
[google/fonts `ofl/charissil`](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/charissil). Charis SIL is
designed for IPA and positions combining marks (the nasal tilde in ɔ̃ ɑ̃ ɛ̃ œ̃) with OpenType anchors.

It was subset with fontTools `pyftsubset` to the characters used by the bundled IPA dictionary
(a–z, IPA letters, U+0303 COMBINING TILDE, ː, ʼ, comma, space), keeping all layout features. As a
Modified Version under the SIL Open Font License it is renamed and does not use the Reserved Font
Names "Charis" or "SIL". License: [OFL.txt](OFL.txt) (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
